# A Presentation is a deck of Boards, with Focus Steps for zooming

A Presentation is an ordered list of Boards with Transitions between them, and a Focus Step zooms the view onto part of the current Board. We chose this over a single infinite canvas with Frames (the Prezi model) because teachers and course creators already think in slides and lessons map cleanly onto Boards, and one canvas becomes unwieldy past a few dozen Elements; Focus Steps still give explainers cinematic push-in moves.

## Consequences

- The Presentation, not a single Board, is what gets recorded, Synced, shared and Remixed.
- Themes and Templates apply to whole Presentations.
