# Pro is $20/month, with a $17 Founding Price locked in for early subscribers

Pro's list price is $20/month. Anyone who subscribes during the launch window (the first 3 months after launch or the first 500 subscribers, whichever comes first) pays the $17 Founding Price for as long as they stay subscribed. We rejected a time-limited sale because ending it means raising prices on existing customers, which caused Loom's 2025 backlash, and rejected a discount on each subscriber's first months because it teaches people to cancel and re-subscribe.

Yearly billing is two months free: $200 a year at list price, $170 a year on the Founding Price. Pro includes 1,000 Credits a month, granted monthly on yearly plans too.

## Consequences

- The Founding Price is a promise: it is never raised for a subscriber who stays subscribed.
- Cancelling forfeits it; a returning subscriber pays the list price then in force.
- In the billing provider (Lemon Squeezy, ADR 0014), Founding subscribers sit on a separate $17 price that stops being offered when the window closes.
